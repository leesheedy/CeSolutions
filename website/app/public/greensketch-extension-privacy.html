Privacy Policy: Clean Energy Solutions for GreenSketch

Chrome extension. Last updated 6 October 2026.

"Clean Energy Solutions for GreenSketch" is a browser extension used by staff of Clean Energy Solutions (CES), 79 Elgin Boulevard, Wodonga VIC 3690 ("we", "us") to prepare quotes in GreenSketch. This policy explains what information the extension handles.

Summary

  • The extension only runs on www.greensketch.ai.
  • Settings and the activity log are stored in your own browser and are not sent to us or anyone else.
  • The only information sent outside your browser is a project's street address, which is sent to OpenStreetMap services to work out a driving distance. You can switch this off.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share information.

Information the extension reads

When you have a GreenSketch quote open, the extension reads the following from that page so it can fill in quantities for you:

  • the project's street address,
  • the number and wattage of solar panels in the design, and the system size,
  • the line items in the quote's "Balance of System" and "Additional Charges" sections.

It does not read customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details or your GreenSketch password.

Information stored in your browser

The following is saved using Chrome's local extension storage, on the computer you are using:

  • Settings: which features are switched on, any rate prices you have changed, and the travel start point.
  • Calculator state: the rates currently selected and their quantities.
  • Activity log: the last 400 actions the extension took, including the project number, project street address, a link to the quote, and the lines and prices added.

This information is not transmitted to us. You can clear the activity log from the extension's settings page, and removing the extension deletes all of it.

Information sent to third parties

To calculate the travel charge, the extension works out the driving distance to the project. This involves two external services:

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation The project's street address, to convert it to map coordinates. No customer name or other details. When you select a travel rate in the calculator, or look up a new travel start point in settings.
OSRM routing service (router.project-osrm.org) Two sets of map coordinates: the travel start point and the project location. When you select a travel rate in the calculator.

As with any web request, these services also receive your IP address. Their handling of requests is covered by their own policies (see the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy at osmfoundation.org). To stop these requests entirely, turn off "Work out travel km from the project address" in the extension's settings; travel kilometres can then be typed in by hand.

The extension makes no other external requests.

What the extension changes in GreenSketch

When you click "Add to quote", the extension adds or updates line items on the open quote, and may create matching entries in your company's GreenSketch item list. That information is held by GreenSketch under your company's GreenSketch account and GreenSketch's own privacy policy. The extension also changes how GreenSketch looks in your browser (logo and hidden menu items); this affects only your own browser.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the extension's single purpose: helping staff build quotes in GreenSketch. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to assess creditworthiness, and do not transfer it to anyone other than the two services described above.

Changes to this policy

If the extension changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
