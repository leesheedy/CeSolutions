Reviewed 14 September 2026. Scope: the six-page Higgsfield redesign, the supplied homepage copy, CES source imagery, and the ThrillX reference. This report distinguishes implemented fixes from hosting and operational work still required.

## Update — 19 September 2026: art direction pass — "One Day"

Copy untouched at the user's direction ("the copy on the site right now is really good but the design needs to be improved"). This is purely how it looks.

**The concept.** CES installs most systems in a single day, and a day is the whole unit of solar. So the site is lit by a real day: `src/site/sun.tsx` runs a NOAA solar-position calculation for the Wodonga office (36.1214°S, 146.8881°E, via the Australia/Melbourne zone so AEST/AEDT is always right) and writes `--sun-el`, `--sun-az`, `--daylight`, `--sky-a/b`, `--solar` and `--shadow-x/y` onto `<html>`, plus a `data-phase` of night/dawn/day/dusk. ~2 KB, no dependencies, recalculated every five minutes. The governing rule is **one light, one clock**: if something moves and it isn't the day passing, it doesn't move.

**What the engine drives.** The hero carries a computed sky wash — strong before dawn, almost nothing at noon — over a warm hairline horizon that brightens as the sun drops. Every card, photo and panel shares one shadow direction taken from the real solar azimuth, settling to a neutral after dark when there is no sun to cast one. The hero glow takes the sun's own colour through atmosphere (deep orange at the horizon, pale gold overhead). A hairline "sun line" on the right margin carries the day's position and the Wodonga time; it is fixed, decorative, desktop-only and costs no layout.

**Typography.** Inter is gone. **Archivo** (one variable file, width 75–125 and weight 400–800) now carries display and body: headings run expanded and heavy — width 125 / weight 800 — which reads as signage on a shed or lettering on a work vehicle rather than the serif-on-cream direction every premium brief defaults to. The width axis is responsive: 125 on desktop, 108 at 860 px, 100 on phones, because expanded type costs lines on a small screen. **JetBrains Mono** carries the instrument voice — timestamps, coordinates, source credits, step rails, breadcrumbs, footer small print.

*Corrected on the first pass:* mono was initially applied to the big display figures too (the trust strip, the estimator result). At 44 px it read as a terminal, not an instrument. Large numbers went back to Archivo with tabular figures — measured, not code — and mono is now reserved for small labels and readouts. Same pass pinned the label amber to a fixed `--amber` so kickers stop turning alarm-orange when the live sun colour shifts after dark.

**Colour, re-roled.** Every dark section moved from flat black to `--night #070C11`, a blue-biased pre-dawn sky. Colour now has jobs: **amber** marks time and measurement (kickers, the sun line, data labels — `#8A5B00` on light for AA, `#FFB000` on dark), **cyan** stays electricity only (CTAs, live states), **mint** stays value you keep (savings, storage, confirmations). The corner-bracket kicker was extended into a surveyor mark that also tops each trust-strip figure.

**Validation.** Build, prerender and typecheck clean. Playwright across ten routes × 320/390/768/1440: no overflow, no stuck headings, no broken images, no console errors. Gallery packs densely (9 tiles, 4 rows). Stylesheet 122 KB; the font swap is roughly weight-neutral (Archivo + JetBrains Mono replace the two Inter faces).
